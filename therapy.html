Who it’s for

  • Moms feeling overwhelmed, anxious, or burned out by the demands of motherhood.
  • Couples who love each other but feel like roommates, and are craving connection.
  • Women coping with life’s changes who want to shift from burned out to balanced.
  • Expecting couples who want to prepare for how their relationship may change.

I offer both coaching and therapy, depending on your location and needs.

How I work

I take a person-centered approach, which means I believe you are the expert in your life. I work with you to develop the tools to troubleshoot your life, and give you a safe space where you can work through anything and everything.

CBT

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy is all about how our thoughts affect our feelings, which affect our actions. We work to understand them, challenge them, and change them when needed.

SFBT

Solution-Focused Brief Therapy is about building awareness and implementing change relatively quickly, so you can start seeing results sooner.

Mindfulness

You don’t realize how helpful taking a moment to step back and be present can be until you try it. We’ll learn many ways to practice this.

Online, from wherever you are

How does right from your couch while dinner is in the oven sound? Or on your lunch break? Online therapy is especially great for busy moms and professionals: more scheduling flexibility, no traffic, no waiting for trains.

We talk through HIPAA-compliant video software, so it feels a lot like FaceTime, but far more secure. All you need is a phone, tablet, or computer with a camera and an internet connection.

Your free 15-minute call
