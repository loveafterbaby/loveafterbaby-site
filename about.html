About

Hi there. I’m Sheina.

I’m a wife, mom of four, licensed therapist, and Gottman Bringing Baby Home Educator—the therapist behind Thrive and Shine Wellness and the wife and mom behind @loveafterbaby.

I’ve been where you are.

You’d think that with all my professional experience, I’d have marriage after baby all figured out, right? Ha! I learned quickly that even relationship “experts” still have to do the work—especially after a baby turns your world upside down.

After having my first, I remember thinking, why didn’t anyone tell me how much our relationship would change? The sleepless nights, the mental load, the constant give, give, give—it wasn’t just overwhelming. At times it felt so lonely.

Then I started noticing that almost every mom I knew felt the same way—my clients in therapy, my friends, the moms at the park, the ones venting in Instagram DMs. Struggle doesn’t mean failure. It means an opportunity to reconnect in a new way.

I took what I knew from my professional training, mixed it with real-life trial and error, and started teaching moms simple, doable ways to rebuild connection—without adding one more thing to their already full plates. No fluff. No guilt. Just practical, realistic strategies.

Are you ready to stop “shoulding” yourself?
