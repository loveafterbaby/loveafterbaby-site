Having children changes everything.

Before kids, you and your partner had all the time and energy in the world for one another. But now your focus has shifted toward your little ones—and away from the two of you.

  • You’re talking less and arguing more.
  • You’re resentful of how imbalanced your responsibilities are.
  • You feel emotionally far apart.

You can be more than roommates again.
